# FigureNest linkable resources and outreach

FigureNest should earn references by making its calculator methods easy to inspect, not by buying links or making claims about endorsements.

## Linkable resources

- Calculator pages with visible formulas, worked examples, assumptions, and limitations.
- The methodology page as a stable explanation of validation, units, and rounding.
- Practical guide clusters that connect a real decision to the relevant calculators.
- The construction, salary/overtime, vehicle-energy, business-pricing, and unit-conversion guides as concise educational references.

## Quality-first outreach

1. Identify a small number of genuinely relevant resource pages maintained by educators, trade organizations, community programs, and independent publications.
2. Review each destination manually before contacting its editor. Suggest only the one FigureNest calculator or guide that directly improves the existing resource.
3. Describe the visible formula, worked example, or limitation that makes the page useful. Do not promise reciprocal links, payment, rankings, or endorsements.
4. Track outreach privately outside the public site. Never add organization names, logos, testimonials, or “featured by” claims unless FigureNest has documented permission.
5. Recheck linked tools after material formula or URL changes so references remain accurate.

Success should be measured by relevant editorial references and useful referral visits, not message volume.
