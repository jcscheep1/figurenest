- Credit Card and Credit Card Payoff share the Credit Card calculator.
- Debt Consolidation and Debt Payoff share the Debt Consolidation calculator.

**Why:** Each intent pair uses the same core inputs, assumptions, and educational context. A mode within one substantial canonical page prevents competing routes and keeps validation, disclosures, FAQs, and links synchronized.

**How to apply:** Extend the existing calculator mode when adding payout or payoff behavior. Do not create a second canonical route unless the method, audience, and content become materially distinct.
