---
name: Health intent boundaries
description: Durable consolidation and exclusion rules for broad Health and Fitness calculator searches.
---

Generic weight remains a unit-conversion intent. Healthy-weight and overweight searches belong on the adult BMI page only when screening limitations and a height-based reference interval are visible. Carbohydrate, protein, and macro searches can share one transparent macronutrient calculator. Conception, ovulation, and period searches can share one regular-cycle estimator with an explicit contraception warning.

Do not publish a broad body-type calculator without a clearly defined, scientifically validated method and responsible interpretation. Do not reproduce branded or proprietary nutrition point systems without established authorization and a verified independent legal basis.

**Why:** Broad health labels can turn weak assumptions into apparent diagnoses, while branded point systems create avoidable scientific and legal risk. Consolidation is appropriate only when the same inputs, equation, population, and limitations genuinely answer the related intents.

**How to apply:** Before adding a Health tool or keyword landing page, identify the exact validated method, intended population, exclusions, safety language, and authoritative sources. Reject or narrow intents that cannot meet that standard.
